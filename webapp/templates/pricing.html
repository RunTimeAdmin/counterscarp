Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after I purchase? ▼
After payment, you'll be redirected to a success page with your license key. You can activate it immediately by setting the SENTINEL_PRO_LICENSE environment variable or adding it to your sentinel.toml config file.
How do I activate my license? ▼
There are two ways to activate:

Option 1 — Environment Variable:
export SENTINEL_PRO_LICENSE=SE-PRO-xxxxx

Option 2 — Config File (sentinel.toml):
[license]
key = "SE-PRO-xxxxx"
Can I cancel my subscription? ▼
Yes. You can cancel anytime — manage your subscription through the Stripe Customer Portal . Your license will remain active until the end of the current billing period. After that, you'll revert to the free tier automatically.
How many machines can I activate? ▼
Each Pro license supports up to 2 machine activations. Each activation is tied to one computer (identified by a hardware fingerprint). For example, your work laptop + home laptop = 2 activations. If you need more, contact us about our Enterprise plan at support@sentinel-engine.io .
Is there a refund policy? ▼
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, contact support@sentinel-engine.io for a full refund.
