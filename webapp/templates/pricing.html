Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Developer and Pro? ▼
Developer gives you access to the web app (5 scans/month), Solana Analyzer, and advanced report formats (HTML/SARIF). Pro unlocks all AI-powered features including the AI Audit Copilot, Attack Graph Visualization, Exploit PoC Generator, Time-Travel Scanner, and Protocol Fingerprinting. Pro also includes unlimited web app scans, 3 machine activations (vs 1), and priority email support with a 24-hour SLA.
What are machine activations? ▼
Each activation is tied to one computer (identified by a hardware fingerprint). This allows you to use your license on multiple machines. For example, with Developer you get 1 activation (one machine), with Pro you get 3 activations (e.g., work laptop, home desktop, and a VM), and Team gives you 10 activations to share across your team. Enterprise includes unlimited activations.
Can I upgrade from Developer to Pro? ▼
Yes! You can upgrade at any time. When you upgrade, we'll prorate your remaining subscription time and apply it to your new plan. Simply purchase the Pro plan and your license will be automatically upgraded.
Do you offer a free trial? ▼
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you're not satisfied, contact support@sentinel-engine.io for a full refund within 14 days of purchase. You can also use the Community tier completely free forever to evaluate the core analyzers.
How do I cancel? ▼
You can cancel anytime through the Stripe Customer Portal. Your license will remain active until the end of the current billing period, after which you'll automatically revert to the Community tier.
What about Enterprise? ▼
Enterprise is designed for large organizations with specific security and compliance needs. It includes everything in Team plus unlimited seats, self-hosted deployment options, custom rule packs, 99.9% uptime SLA, a dedicated customer success manager, on-site training, and custom integrations with your existing tools (SIEM, Jira, Linear). Contact support@sentinel-engine.io to discuss your requirements.
What happens after I purchase? ▼
After payment, you'll be redirected to a success page with your license key. You can activate it immediately by setting the SENTINEL_PRO_LICENSE environment variable or adding it to your sentinel.toml config file.
How do I activate my license? ▼
There are two ways to activate:

Option 1 — Environment Variable:
export SENTINEL_PRO_LICENSE=SE-PRO-xxxxx

Option 2 — Config File (sentinel.toml):
[license]
key = "SE-PRO-xxxxx"
